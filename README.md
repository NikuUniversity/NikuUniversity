# Niku University Online

## MBA in Marketing Management (UAE & Germany Focus)

Welcome to **Niku University Online**. This repository and documentation guide serves as the foundational overview for our premier MBA in Marketing program, specifically designed to bridge strategic business practices between Europe and the Middle East.

Leveraging our advanced e-learning infrastructure developed in partnership with **Niku Software**, we deliver an immersive, flexible, and high-impact educational experience for ambitious professionals.

---

### 🌍 Target Markets & Regional Focus

While our digital campus is accessible globally, this program features specialized modules tailored for the dynamic economic corridors of Germany and the **Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC)**. 

Students will analyze case studies and develop market entry strategies specifically for the following Gulf countries:

*   **Saudi Arabia**
*   **United Arab Emirates (UAE)**
*   **Kuwait**
*   **Qatar**
*   **Bahrain**
*   **Oman**

---

### 📚 Program Highlights

Our curriculum is built for the modern digital economy. Key focus areas include:
*   **International Marketing Strategy:** Cross-border campaign management and localized consumer behavior.
*   **Digital & Social Media Management:** Building business presence across Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and emerging platforms.
*   **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Analytics, SEO, and performance tracking.
*   **Agile Team Leadership:** Managing distributed teams and utilizing AI-assisted workflows.
